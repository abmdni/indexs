Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15-08-2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SinkFund Tracker ("the app," "we," "our") handles information when you use the app.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

SinkFund Tracker is designed to work entirely offline. All information you enter — fund names, target amounts, target dates, and contribution history — is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate a server, and we do not have access to this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes this information; we cannot recover it for you.

The app offers an optional "Export data" feature that creates a file containing your data for you to save or share at your own discretion. This export is created and shared entirely by you — we do not receive a copy.

2. Advertising (AdMob)

SinkFund Tracker displays ads served by Google AdMob to support free use of the app. AdMob may collect device information, advertising identifiers, and general usage data to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. You can learn more about how Google handles this data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

For users in the UK and European Economic Area, the app presents a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before loading ads, allowing you to control ad personalization in accordance with applicable regulations.

3. No Account or Sign-In

The app does not require or offer account creation, sign-in, or any form of user authentication. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifying information directly.

4. Notifications

If you enable contribution reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated and delivered entirely on-device and are not sent through any external server.

5. Children's Privacy

SinkFund Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at unidayproduction@gmail.com.
